NaptimeOffice Pin Scheduler

NaptimeOffice Pin Scheduler is a small private tool used only by the shop owner. It schedules and posts Pins that the owner has reviewed and approved, sharing the shop's own product images to the owner's own NaptimeOffice Pinterest business account. It stores only what it needs to work (see the Privacy Policy).

  • Single owner: it connects only the owner's own Pinterest business account. There are no other users.
  • Standard OAuth: it signs in through Pinterest's OAuth 2.0 authorization flow. No passwords or session cookies are used.
  • Own content only: it creates organic Pins of the shop's original product images, each linking to the matching Etsy listing.
  • Low volume: at most about 6 Pins per week, never duplicates.
  • No other users' data and no ads: it does not read, collect or store anyone else's data, and it does not follow, comment, message or run ads.
  • Scopes (5): boards:read, boards:write, pins:read, pins:write, user_accounts:read. boards:write is used only to create and manage the owner's own boards (for example a temporary test board for the app demo, or a board to organize the shop's Pins).

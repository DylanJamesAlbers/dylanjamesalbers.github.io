Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 21, 2026

This policy covers this website and the NaptimeOffice Pins tool. Purchases made on Etsy are also covered by Etsy's own privacy policy.

This website

This site does not use cookies, analytics or forms, and it does not collect personal information.

NaptimeOffice Pins: what it reads and writes

  • Reads: the owner's own Pinterest account details (to confirm the correct business account is connected), the owner's boards (to find the destination board), and Pins the tool itself created (to confirm they posted).
  • Writes: new organic Pins of the shop's own product images on the owner's boards.

What is stored

The only Pinterest data stored is the OAuth access and refresh tokens for the owner's own account, kept in a local file readable only by the owner (permission 0600) on the owner's computer. Tokens are never printed, logged or shared. Board IDs and names and the IDs of Pins the tool created are kept in a local log to prevent duplicate posts.

What is not done

No data from any other Pinterest user is accessed, collected or stored. No Pinterest data is sold, shared, or used for advertising or analytics.

How to revoke access

Access can be revoked at any time in Pinterest under Settings → Security and logins → Apps (connected apps) by removing NaptimeOffice Pins. After revocation the local token files are deleted.
